Client profile generator

For NGO staff only. Responses are read on this computer and are never uploaded.

Load the questionnaire responses to build a client profile

  1. Open the questionnaire's response sheet and select the Form Responses 1 tab.
  2. Choose File, then Download, then Comma-separated values (.csv).
  3. Click Load responses (CSV) above and pick that file, then choose a client.

Each profile is a staff briefing: it tells you how to approach the client. Don't share it with the client as is.
